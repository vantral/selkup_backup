## About the collection

The corpus brings together legal texts translated into the Upper Taz variety of Northern Selkup. The materials are associated with publications prepared in Salekhard in 2008 and permit comparison of Selkup translations with their Russian originals.

## Contents and search

- parallel Selkup and Russian texts;
- word-form and lemma search;
- grammatical and morpheme-level annotation;
- document metadata and full-text display.

Legal terminology and some glosses are specific to this collection. Automatically inferred explanations ending in a question mark require confirmation by the translators.
